Who We Are

Kakkar Enterprises is a leading dealer of electric vehicles in Chandigarh, India. We specialize in the sale, service, repair, and spare parts of all types of electric vehicles including e-scooters, e-rickshaws, e-loaders, and e-cycles. With years of experience in the EV industry, we are committed to providing our customers with high-quality products, exceptional service, and competitive prices. Our team of certified technicians ensures that every vehicle we sell and service meets the highest standards of quality and performance. We also offer flexible financing options and comprehensive insurance coverage to make your EV ownership experience smooth and hassle-free.
