WELCOME TO KAKKAR ENTERPRISES

We believe that exceptional electric vehicle care goes hand in hand with providing top-quality pre-owned vehicles. That's why we offer a comprehensive range of services to ensure that your beloved ride remains in pristine condition. Pamper your electric vehicle with us.

1. Electric Vehicle Servicing: Expert Care for Optimal Performance

Regular vehicle servicing is essential to keep your EV running smoothly and to maximize its lifespan. Our team of highly skilled technicians is well-versed in servicing a wide variety of electric vehicle makes and models. From routine battery checks and tire rotations to more complex motor diagnostics and repairs, we take every step to ensure that your vehicle performs at its best.

2. Detailing Body Wash: Get the Gloss of Showroom Electric Vehicles

Your electric vehicle deserves the royal treatment, and our detailing body wash service guarantees a showroom-worthy finish. Our skilled detailers work diligently to remove dirt, grime, and scratches, revealing your vehicle's true radiance. We utilize top-tier products that protect your vehicle's paint, leaving it with a stunning shine that will turn heads on the road.

3. Body Shop: Restoring Beauty and Safety

Accidents happen, and when they do, our state-of-the-art body shop team is here to make your electric vehicle look as good as new. Our certified technicians have extensive experience in handling all types of body repairs, from minor dings to major collisions. Using advanced techniques and high-quality materials, we meticulously restore your vehicle's body to its original condition.
